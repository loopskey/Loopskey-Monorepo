## OAuth onboarding parity (added after the initial run)

The onboarding decision lives in `ProfessionalOnboardingGate`, keyed on
`professionalDashboardProfile.onboardingCompletedAt`, and both authentication
methods already land on `/dashboard/professional` — so the decision itself was
never email/password specific.

The real divergence was provisioning: `RoleProfileRegistry.provision()` ran in
`auth-registration.service.ts` (and admin/org creation) but **not** in either
OAuth service, so OAuth-created accounts had no `ProfessionalProfile` row.

- New `AuthOAuthProvisioningService` pairs `user.create` with
  `roleProfiles.provision` in one transaction, mirroring the email/password
  path, and exposes `ensureRoleProfile` for accounts predating the fix.
- Google and LinkedIn both use it; LinkedIn's `AuthAccount` write moved into the
  same transaction via a `link` callback.
- Provisioning upserts on `userId`, so retried callbacks cannot duplicate.
- No contract change: `schema.gql` and generated types are unchanged.
- Google still resolves identity by email and creates no `AuthAccount`, unlike
  LinkedIn's stable `providerUserId`. Left as-is deliberately — changing it
  alters authentication semantics the brief required to keep intact.
